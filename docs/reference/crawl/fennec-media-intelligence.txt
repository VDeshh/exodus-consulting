 Channel readiness is shown before the send, not after it bounces. The audience comes from the guest profiles the venue already owns, the tokens fill from the same records, and the phone on the right previews what lands in Gmail, Outlook or Apple Mail.

The record underneath 
One conversation per guest, and seven fields that fill themselves. 
A venue usually answers the same guest in three places: an email thread, a text on somebody's phone, and a push notification nobody can find again. The unified inbox threads email, SMS and push into one conversation per guest, so the next person to pick it up can see what was already promised. Our description, not a product line. 
The same records drive personalisation. The composer offers seven tokens, and every one of them reads a field the venue already holds on the guest profile rather than a column pasted into a spreadsheet. 
 Where the guest record comes from 
 First name 
 The given name on the guest profile. 
 Last name 
 The family name on the same profile. 
 VIP tier 
 The tier the venue has assigned to that guest. 
 Visit count 
 How many times they have been through the door. 
 Lifetime spend 
 What they have spent across those visits. 
 Last visit 
 The date they were last in the room. 
 Birthday month 
 The month to send against, without asking anyone to keep a list.

Ferry AI 
A roster, not a chat box. 
One general assistant pointed at a venue's data is a search box with a friendly tone. Ferry AI splits the job by surface and gives each surface an owner, which is the only version of this that can be tested and corrected one piece at a time. 
 FIG. 03 
 Screenshot, Ferry AI assistant 
 The Ferry AI panel and its agent roster. Each agent owns a surface of the operation rather than answering everything badly, which is why an operator asking about last Saturday's tables gets an answer from the agent that holds the tables. 
 The roster 
 Sales Analytics, Event Management, Table and Bottle, Guestlist and Reservations, Promos and Insights. 
 Why a network 
 One general assistant over a venue's data is a search box. A roster with an owner per surface can be tested, corrected and trusted per surface. 
 What it reads 
 The same records the other families write: events, tables, tabs, stock counts, guest profiles and campaign results. 
 What it writes 
 Automations. A signal in the data triggers an SMS, WhatsApp or email flow without anyone watching a dashboard for it. 
The Revenue Copilot 
 Live 
It analyses voids, pricing, promoters and inventory. Those four are on the list because they are where the money goes missing quietly: a void nobody queries, a price that drifted, a promoter whose guests never spend, a bottle count that never matched the pour. Same records, read on purpose. 
Beyond nightlife 
A campaign engine on top of your own records is not a nightlife idea. 
These two families are shaped like a nightclub because that is the room they were built in. The parts underneath are not. A guest profile is a customer record. A token is a merge field that never goes stale. An agent roster is a set of narrow assistants over data you already own, each one narrow enough to be checked.
