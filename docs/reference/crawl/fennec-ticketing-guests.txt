Ticketing 
"Sell tickets, import guestlists, and check guests in with a scanner." 
One record holds the event. The scanner at the door reads against the same record the promoter sold from, so a guestlist import does not become a second source of truth. 
Live across 20+ event companies 
 The public side of the same event: date, age limit, venue address, presenter, share actions, and two ways to commit. 
Public event page 
The guest-facing face of the event record. Our words, not a product line. 
Reserve a table or join the guestlist, from the page the operator just built. Nothing is retyped into a second tool, which is why the address on the page is never last season's address. 
Live across the Fennec venue network 
 A featured event above a grid of what is coming up, assembled from every venue on the network rather than one venue's calendar. 
Discover Events 
"A guest-facing feed of every event across the Fennec network." 
A venue that joins the network inherits an audience already inside the app. The feed is the reason a guest who came for one room finds the next one. 
Network wide 
 The guest's own profile: upcoming tickets, table and guestlist counts, a next-up countdown, the Pass QR they enter with, and a city feed underneath. 
Fennec Pass 
"A guest's universal nightlife identity - tickets, table bookings, loyalty tiers." 
One QR, every venue. Loyalty is configured once and the passes issue themselves, so a tier earned at one room is recognised at the next. 
Live across the Fennec venue network 
The night in order 
Six steps, one profile, no retyping. 
This is the path a guest actually walks. Each step writes to the same guest record, which is the only reason the venue still knows who that person is a month later. 
 1 
The event goes up 
 Operator, once 
Poster, slug, venue, schedule, age restriction and the guestlist scan cutoff go in once, in the event builder, with the customer view previewed alongside. 
 2 
Promoters get their own link 
 Promoter Portal 
Dedicated dashboards, personal links, guestlist tools and promo codes. Sales are attributed to the person who made them instead of being argued about on Monday. 
 3 
The guest commits 
 Public event page 
A ticket, a table reservation or a guestlist request, taken on the page the operator just built. RSVPs, comps, plus-ones and birthdays are handled in Guest Management rather than in a group chat. 
 4 
The door scans 
 Night of 
The scanner reads against the same event record. An imported guestlist and a sold ticket check in through one queue, so the count at the door matches the count in the office. 
 5 
The table reorders 
 Live Ordering 
Guests scan their table, browse the live menu, and reorder. Spend lands on the guest profile as it happens instead of being reconstructed from a stack of receipts. 
 6 
The profile keeps 
 Guest CRM and Loyalty 
Spend history, preferences and segments sit on one profile. Tiers, points and perks are configured once, and the pass a guest earned at one room is recognised at the next.

Beyond nightlife 
An event page is a booking funnel with a scanner at the end. 
This family is shaped like a nightclub because that is the room it was built in. The parts underneath are not. A guest profile is a customer record. A promoter link is an attributed sales channel. A loyalty tier is a retention programme. A scanner at the door is admission control for anything with a gate.
