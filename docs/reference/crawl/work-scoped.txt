Why this page exists 
A build in progress is not a launch, and we will not write it as one. 
Most consulting portfolios flatten everything into one tense, so a system that went live last year reads the same as one that is still being written. Ours does not. Five engagements sit across this practice: two live, one shipped and two in build, every one of them real work with a client. The two flagship studies have their own pages, and this page holds the other two so the difference between running and being built stays visible. 
ABC is live, and what we describe is the work we do rather than any outcome at the ballot box. B-Side Group is under construction: real work, no launch metric yet. Where there is no number, we say so instead of borrowing one.

Engagement 01 
ABC Live 
ABC is a municipal political party in Vancouver. We parse and reconcile the records they already hold, matching them across sources and scoring each one for confidence, so it is clear what can be relied on. Their targeted marketing campaigns are then planned against that reconciled set rather than several disagreeing lists. 
The parsing and the reconciliation are the bulk of the work. An organisation that has grown in stages ends up holding its information in whatever tool each part of the operation happened to adopt, so the same person can exist three times in three formats, with a different spelling and a stale address on each. We read every source into a common shape, match the records across them, resolve the duplicates and the near-duplicates, and attach a confidence score to each match so a weak join stays visible instead of being quietly trusted. It is unglamorous work and it is most of the value: outreach built on unreconciled records spends its budget arguing with the data instead of reaching people. 
It is live. We claim no electoral or polling outcome here, we take no position on the party or its policies, and nothing on this page should be read as the party endorsing us. What we are accountable for is the parsing and reconciliation work and the campaign marketing support, and that is the whole of what we describe.

Engagement 02 
B-Side Group In build 
B-Side Group is a hospitality group. We are building them customised inventory management software across three of their restaurant venues. 
Inventory is where a multi-venue group loses money quietly. Each room counts its own stock its own way, the same product carries three names across three sites, and the gap between what was bought and what was sold only surfaces at the end of the month, by which time it is history rather than a decision. Software of this kind is our answer to that: stock counts done per site, one catalogue shared across the venues, purchased against sold set side by side as variance, and reorder points that fire off real consumption rather than a hunch. 
It is in build, so read that paragraph as the shape of the build and not as a delivered result. No count is running in our software yet, no variance figure exists, and there is no launch metric or screenshot to show. When there is one, it will appear with its arithmetic next to it, the same as everything else on this site.
